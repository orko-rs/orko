# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of behavior required of all
participants in the orko project and the procedures by which those standards
are enforced. Its objective is a harassment-free environment in
which contributions are evaluated solely on their merit.

## 2. Scope

This Code applies to all project spaces maintained under the
[orko-rs](https://github.com/orko-rs) GitHub organization: repositories,
issue trackers, pull requests, code review, and discussions; and to any
individual officially representing the project in a public forum. Conduct
outside these spaces falls within scope where it has a direct and
demonstrable impact on a participant's safety or ability to take part in the
project.

## 3. Expected Conduct

All participants shall:

1. treat other participants with courtesy and respect;
2. direct criticism at technical substance, never at persons;
3. give feedback in good faith and receive it in the same spirit;
4. acknowledge and correct their own errors; and
5. act in the interest of the project and its community as a whole.

## 4. Prohibited Conduct

The following constitute violations of this Code:

1. harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form, including on the
   basis of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression,
   nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
   orientation;
2. sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention of any kind;
3. insulting, derogatory, or ad hominem remarks; trolling; and personal or
   political attacks;
4. publication of another person's private information, including physical
   or electronic addresses, without that person's explicit consent;
5. sustained disruption of discussion, review, or any other project
   activity; and
6. any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate
   in a professional setting.

## 5. Reporting

Suspected violations shall be reported privately to the maintainers of the
orko-rs organization; they must not be raised as public issues, discussions,
or pull requests. A report should identify the parties involved, describe
the conduct at issue, state where and when it occurred, and attach any
supporting material.

Reports are acknowledged within 7 days. The identity of a reporter is
treated as confidential and is disclosed only with the reporter's consent.
A maintainer who is a party to a reported incident, or otherwise holds a
conflict of interest in it, is recused from its investigation.

## 6. Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for the interpretation and enforcement of
this Code and shall investigate every report promptly and impartially.
Sanctions are proportionate to the severity and recurrence of the violation
and comprise, in escalating order:

1. **Private reprimand.** A written statement of the violation and of the
   conduct expected henceforth.
2. **Formal warning.** A written warning, which may impose restrictions on
   interaction with affected parties for a stated period. Breach of such
   restrictions escalates to exclusion.
3. **Temporary exclusion.** Suspension from all project spaces for a stated
   period. Any circumvention of the suspension escalates it to permanent
   exclusion.
4. **Permanent exclusion.** Permanent removal from all project spaces.

Independently of the above, the maintainers may remove, edit, or reject any
contribution: comment, commit, issue, or pull request that violates this
Code, and will state their reasons where practicable.
